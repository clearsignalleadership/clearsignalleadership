Curriculum

What you will learn

Attention is our greatest power. In this course, I'll introduce five practices that changed my life, practices that helped me bring my attention back from pattern to inner authority.

Each session, we'll explore one or two simple yet powerful presencing practices through meditation, visualization, self-reflection, and interactive exercises. This work is 20% intellectual and 80% somatic, training your attention, your embodied knowing, and collecting data on the automatic ways we lose ourselves and leave our center.

You'll leave with a deeper understanding of your own system, and simple yet powerful tools to play with in your everyday life.

Your biggest life doesn't write itself.
You need to choose it.

Week 1

My Core, My Center

Who you are, underneath the roles. Coming back to your own ground.

Week 2

Grounded & Resourced

The ground you actually stand on, and the resources that hold you when things move.

Week 3

Me, Not Me

Untangling what's actually yours from what you've been carrying that was never yours to begin with.

Week 4

My Space & Boundaries

Claiming your space fully: your power, your shine, your aliveness. And a felt-sense introduction to boundaries, sensed and held, not just stated.

Week 5

Beyond My Pattern

Where does your automatic attention go when you're stressed or overwhelmed? Which pattern do you run, and what does it cost you?

Week 6

Visioning & New Beginnings

A closing ceremony. Naming what you're taking with you, and envisioning how you'll live, lead, and create from this new place.
